## Usage review — 2026-10-19 KST

The table above records the **previous implementation's source claims**, not a grant of permission.
Most rows name a site and image role but do not retain the exact asset URL, original download,
license text, or permission from the owner. Treat those rows as **permission unverified**.
Do not describe these files as licensed, approved by the brands, or proof of a partnership.
Rehosting an image and receiving HTTP 200 do not establish reuse rights.

- [medicube official terms](https://medicube.us/pages/terms-of-service): Content and Marks are
  proprietary; no commercial redistribution permission was established in this review.
- [COSRX official terms](https://www.cosrx.com/policies/terms-of-service): no logo reuse grant
  was established in this review.
- THE SAEM: the earlier table claims CC BY 4.0, but the file page/creator and license could
  not be independently retrieved in this environment. The claim remains unverified. Before
  relying on it, retain the creator, original file URL, [license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/),
  and a change notice (the existing PNG is resized/composed into a round badge). A copyright
  license does not itself resolve trademark use.
- All other rows: permission and exact asset provenance remain unverified. The linked site
  alone is insufficient evidence. Retain the original asset URL, retrieved date, owner,
  applicable terms/permission, and changes before marking any row cleared.
